A Petition for Grace

To My Queen

From a foolish, yet deeply devoted, Persian Noble.

O Light of my Eyes,

I come before you today with my head bowed, stripped of all pride and artifice. Like a moth drawn endlessly to the brightest flame, my longing for your presence led my judgment astray.

"Roses as red as the blood that beats in my heart for you."
The Confession

In my desperate eagerness to be near you, to bask in the warmth of your words without revealing the extent of my own vulnerability, I donned a mask. I spoke to you through the guise of another—my friend's account.

It was a deception. But I swear upon the stars that blanket the night sky over Shiraz, it was not born of malice, nor a desire to deceive your noble heart. It was born only of a cowardly, foolish reverence. I was terrified that my own self was not spectacular enough to hold your royal attention.

"Tulips, a symbol of perfect, enduring love laid at your feet."

I lay these roses and tulips at your feet. An offering of my sincerity. I promise, by all that is sacred, to never hide behind another's shadow again. You deserve a lover who stands in the light, unashamed of his devotion.

Can your royal heart find it within to forgive me?

My Queen is Merciful

"Your forgiveness is the gentle rain upon the parched gardens of my soul. From this day forward, I vow to speak to you only in my true voice, for no disguise could ever be worthy of your grace."
